# Product: Clear to Pay for Jira

An AI agent can be fooled into believing an invoice. Clear to Pay makes sure that belief is not enough to move the company's money.

A Forge app for Jira and Jira Service Management. A supplier invoice is a Jira issue. It can move to "Paid" only when every rule passes. Verdicts: CLEARED, HELD (wait or fix; nothing is lost), SEIZED (looks like fraud; blocked and logged). Every refusal returns a named reason code.

Users: buyer (finance), storekeeper, weighbridge operator, supplier (through the JSM portal), approver.

Two themes over one product: "standard" (navy #10264A, gold #E6BE4F) for Codegeist, and "kiroween" (the tattered flag, midnight slate, pumpkin orange) for Kiroween. Themes change labels and colours only, never rules.
